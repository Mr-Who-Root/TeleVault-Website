How it works

Three steps, then it looks after itself.

  1. 01

    Sign in with Telegram

    Use your Telegram phone number and the login code Telegram sends you. The first time, you also add your own free Telegram API ID and API hash (about two minutes; the app walks you through it). Your session stays on your device and never leaves it.

  2. 02

    Add a vault

    Pick a folder (quick start for Camera, Screenshots, WhatsApp and more) and where it should go: your Saved Messages, or a private channel or group you can create right in the app.

  3. 03

    Sync, and it's safe

    Tap Sync, or with Pro let it run on its own. Everything you've backed up shows up in the Cloud tab on every device you sign in on, where you can browse, play and share it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's new in TeleVault 2.0?

2.0 is out now for Free and Pro: a Cloud tab you can browse, play and share from, smart search, Notes, Downloads, Storage and Backup check, faster backups, encryption, App lock and 15 languages, and in Pro also Music, Movies & Series for your own films and shows, Wi-Fi transfer, Import from Google and themes. See What's new above. Already using TeleVault? Update from Google Play, open it and tap Switch to 2.0.

Where does TeleVault send my backups?

Wherever you choose when you set up a vault: usually your own Saved Messages, or a private group or channel you create just for backups. Nothing leaves your Telegram account.

How do I create a private Telegram channel for backups?

Right in TeleVault: wherever you choose where backups go (adding a vault or the Upload tab), tap Create new channel or group, keep Channel selected, give it a name and tap Create. It's made as a private channel with only you in it and selected straight away. Or, in the Telegram app, tap the pencil / new-message icon (or the menu in the top corner), choose New Channel, name it, choose Private and tap Create. You can skip adding members.

How do I create a Telegram group?

Right in TeleVault: wherever you choose where backups go (adding a vault or the Upload tab), tap Create new channel or group, choose Group, give it a name and tap Create. It's made as a private group in your own Telegram account and selected straight away. Or, in the Telegram app, tap the pencil / new-message icon (or the menu in the top corner), choose New Group, add contacts, name it and tap the checkmark.

What's the maximum file size I can back up?

Up to 2 GB per file in TeleVault Free — Telegram's limit for one message. TeleVault Pro has no limit: bigger files are sent in parts and shown as one file. Telegram has no overall storage limit.

What is the "TeleVault Data" channel?

A private channel TeleVault makes in your Telegram account, archived and muted, where it keeps your vault list, file list, settings and (if you use encryption) your passphrase-sealed key backup. It's what lets a new phone or a reinstall get everything back by itself after you sign in. Only you are in it. Please don't delete it; if you do, TeleVault makes a new one.

Are my backed-up files private?

Yes. Files go directly from your device to Telegram through your own account. Nobody else can see them unless you share the chat, group or channel they land in.

How do I get a backed-up file back on my phone?

Tap Restore files on a vault's card to bring its files back, or in the Cloud tab open a file (or long-press to select several) and choose Save to Downloads. Files you sent from the Upload tab have Restore right in their viewer.

Why did a backup get skipped or blocked?

Check Settings → Backup Behavior: a Wi-Fi-only or charging-only setting may be holding it until those conditions are met. Also check the vault card. If it asks you to choose the folder again, Android has dropped the app's access to it.

Is there a Mac, Windows or Linux version?

Yes, for TeleVault Pro: macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows 10/11 and 64-bit Linux. They aren't in an app store. Buy TeleVault Pro on Google Play, then email the developer saying which version you want and the order number from your Google Play receipt, and you'll get the download by email. Sign in with the same Telegram account and your Cloud, vaults and Continue watching are all there.

What does TeleVault Pro add?

Unlimited vaults, automatic backups (when new files are added, every 15 minutes, hourly, daily or weekly), Wi-Fi-only and charging-only backups, up to 6 uploads at once and no file size limit, Music, Movies & Series, Wi-Fi transfer, Import from Google, theme colours and tab animations, the Mac, Windows and Linux apps, and no ads anywhere.

Does TeleVault Free show ads?

Yes — TeleVault Free is free and supported by ads: ads among your lists, a pop-up now and then between tabs (watch two short ads to pause them for a day), and short ads you choose to watch to add a channel or group, make room for another vault or play a video. TeleVault Pro is a paid app with no ads.

I see an error code like "TV-301". What should I do?

Every error message ends with a code, for example "(Error TV-301)". Most errors clear up if you simply try again. If one keeps coming back, email the code and what you were doing to letter2govind@gmail.com so it can be looked into and fixed.

No. TeleVault is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram.

Already using TeleVault?

2.0 signs in with your own free Telegram API ID and API hash, which you get once at my.telegram.org — about two minutes, and the app walks you through it. How it works

Three steps, then it looks after itself.

  1. 01

    Sign in with Telegram

    Use your Telegram phone number and the login code Telegram sends you. Your session stays in your phone's secure storage.

  2. 02

    Add a vault

    Pick a folder and where it should go: your Saved Messages, or a private channel or group you can create right in the app. A short guide on the Vaults tab walks you through it.

  3. 03

    Sync, and it's safe

    Tap Sync, or with Pro let it run on its own. Everything you've backed up shows up in the Cloud tab, ready to restore.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's in TeleVault 2.0, and when is it coming?

2.0 is a whole new TeleVault for Free and Pro: a Cloud tab you can browse, play and share from, Notes, Downloads, Storage and Backup check, faster backups, encryption, App lock and 15 languages, and in Pro also Music, Movies & Series for your own films and shows, Wi-Fi transfer, Import from Google and themes. It's coming to Google Play soon; see TeleVault 2.0 above.

Where does TeleVault send my backups?

Wherever you choose when you set up a vault: usually your own Saved Messages, or a private group or channel you create just for backups. Nothing leaves your Telegram account.

How do I create a private Telegram channel for backups?

Right in TeleVault: wherever you choose where backups go (adding a vault, the Upload tab, or Cloud list backup), tap Create new channel or group, keep Channel selected, give it a name and tap Create. It's made as a private channel with only you in it and selected straight away. Or, in the Telegram app, tap the pencil / new-message icon (or the menu in the top corner), choose New Channel, name it, choose Private and tap Create. You can skip adding members.

How do I create a Telegram group?

Right in TeleVault: wherever you choose where backups go (adding a vault, the Upload tab, or Cloud list backup), tap Create new channel or group, choose Group, give it a name and tap Create. It's made as a private group in your own Telegram account and selected straight away. Or, in the Telegram app, tap the pencil / new-message icon (or the menu in the top corner), choose New Group, add contacts, name it and tap the checkmark.

What's the maximum file size I can back up?

2 GB per file, or 4 GB per file if your Telegram account has Telegram Premium. Telegram has no overall storage limit.

Are my backed-up files private?

Yes. Files go directly from your device to Telegram through your own account. Nobody else can see them unless you share the chat, group or channel they land in.

How do I restore a backed-up file?

Open the Cloud tab, tap a file or long-press to select several, then tap Restore to save them to your phone again.

Why did a backup get skipped or blocked?

Check Settings → Backup Behavior: a Wi-Fi-only or charging-only setting may be holding it until those conditions are met. Also check the vault card. If it asks you to choose the folder again, Android has dropped the app's access to it.

What does TeleVault Pro add?

Unlimited vaults, automatic backups (when new files are added, every 15 minutes, hourly, daily or weekly), Wi-Fi-only and charging-only backups, and no ads anywhere.

I see an error code like "TV-301". What should I do?

Every error message ends with a code, for example "(Error TV-301)". Most errors clear up if you simply try again. If one keeps coming back, email the code and what you were doing to letter2govind@gmail.com so it can be looked into and fixed.

No. TeleVault is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram.
